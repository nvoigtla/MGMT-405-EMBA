How the term runs

The course runs over 12 weeks: 3 on-campus weekends and 5 weeks of video content to watch before the next class, plus a midterm, exam preparation time and the final.

Every week has its own page, reached from the list on the left. Each page shows what to do beforehand and what happens in class. The same material can also be browsed module by module.

Textbook

  • “Microeconomics” by Goolsbee, Levitt, Syverson (4th edition). See the different purchase options in the class syllabus.
  • The “Figure it out” boxes in each chapter should be considered as advanced (voluntary) readings.
  • For additional and optional practice, you can find review questions at the end of each chapter (with answers at the end of the textbook).

Math refresher

If you feel you could use a math refresher before the class, I suggest:

  • Take the Math Quiz (10 – 15 min)
  • Watch the Math Review Videos
  • Textbook reading: Math review Appendix Section 1 + Section 2 (only first derivatives)
  • Achieve: the first section of the Achieve course provides math and graphing video tutorials
