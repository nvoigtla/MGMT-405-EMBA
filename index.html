How the Quarter Runs

The course runs over the fall quarter: We have 3 on-campus weekends. In between those, there is video content to watch before each class, plus a midterm exam and a final exam.

Every week has its own page, reached from the list on the left. The class is organized by 8 Modules, and the class material can also be browsed module by module.
